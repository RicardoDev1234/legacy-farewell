How much does life insurance actually cost?

It depends on your age, health, and coverage amount — but most people are surprised how affordable it is. Final expense plans often start around $1–2 a day, and the younger you apply, the lower your rate locks in. Waiting is what makes it expensive.

Do I need a medical exam?

Often, no. Many final expense and simplified-issue policies skip the exam entirely — just a few health questions. Your agent will tell you exactly what applies to you before anything moves forward.

I'm older or have health issues — can I still qualify?

In most cases, yes. Final expense plans are built for people roughly 50–85, including those with health conditions, and guaranteed-issue options exist too. A quick, free consultation is the only way to know for sure.
